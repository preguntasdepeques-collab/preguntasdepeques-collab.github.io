Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

Preguntas de Peques (preguntasdepeques@gmail.com).

What data the tool uses

Only the access tokens that TikTok, Meta and Google issue when the account owner authorizes the app, and the basic profile information of the owner's own accounts (for TikTok, the user.info.basic scope: open ID, display name and avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected.

Where it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in the Windows Credential Manager. They are never shared or sent to anyone other than the platform that issued them.

What we do not do

We do not collect data from other users, we do not sell or share data, and we do not use it for advertising.

YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in each platform's settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Meta: Business settings → System users; Google: link above), and the tokens are deleted from the computer.
